II.1.4) Short description
In 2019, the Dutch Ministry of the Foreign Affairs has launched PROSPECTS, a multiannual
hybrid project/partnership between five multilateral organisations, with the institutional goal
of furthering the transformation of the ongoing responses to protracted forced displacement in
some of the most affected countries in the world. PROSPECTS brings together organisations
working across the humanitarian-development spectrum: United Nations High Commissioner
for Refugees (UNHCR), United Nations Children’s Fund (UNICEF), International Labour
Organisation (ILO), International Finance Corporation (IFC), and the World Bank (WB).
The Government of the Netherlands has committed to allocate USD 558 million in total for
this partnership over the course of 5 years. PROSPECTS is operational in 8 countries of the
Middle East and the Horn of Africa. The Partnership is currently entering the third year of its
existence with (at least) another two years planned.
