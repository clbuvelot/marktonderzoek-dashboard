5. Lot
5.1 Lot: LOT-0001
Title:  Lot 1: Evaluation services
Description: 
Lot 1: Evaluation services. This is an overarching term for various types of research at the project and 
portfolio levels. More speciﬁcally, it includes the following services: a. Process evaluations: research on the 
implementation, planning, and management of an intervention/project/programme/subsidy framework, 
without speciﬁcally examining eﬀectiveness; b. Ex-ante studies: analysis of expected eﬀects, costs, and 
outcomes of an intervention/project/programme/subsidy frameworkbefore implementation; c. Baseline 
studies: a baseline measurement of speciﬁc indicators at the beginning of an intervention/project
/programme/subsidy framework, before implementation; d. Mid-term reviews: conducting research on the 
implementation process and/or the level of eﬀectiveness and/or eﬃciency, halfway through the duration of 
an intervention/project/programme/subsidy framework; e. Eﬀect evaluations: assessing the eﬀectiveness, 
with a clear causal claim, and eﬃciency of a project, in the ﬁnal year or after completion of a project; f. 
Longer evaluation trajectories: researchers are involved throughout the entire implementation of an 
intervention/project/programme/subsidy framework. This includes providing independent advice in setting 
up MEL systems, conducting baseline studies, mid-term reviews, and ﬁnal (eﬀect) evaluation; g. Portfolio 
evaluation: an evaluation of an instrument/subsidy framework/number of thematically or geographically 
grouped interventions, under which various projects are implemented, possibly in multiple countries; h. 
Policy Evaluation Review (PR); these policy evaluations are largely based on previously completed 
evaluations. For each budget article, insights obtained through evaluations should be compiled at least once 
every four to seven years in a PR (in accordance with Regeling periodiek evaluatieonderzoek 2022).

5.1.10 Award criteria
Criterion:
Type:  Quality
Name:  1. Technical ability and experience in evaluation methods
Description: 
For the Ministry of Foreign Aﬀairs it is important to contract parties that have the technical ability and 
experience in using evaluation methods considered necessary to perform the Contract. Therefore, the 
Tenderer must enclose one evaluation report, and if needed the inception report or separate annexes, 
which will be used to grade the technical ability and experience of the Tenderer. Note that reports will be 
treated conﬁdentially. The submitted report may be among the reports enclosed to assess the eligibility 
requirements (see 3.3). The submitted evaluation report must meet the following requirements: • The 
evaluation report must be in Dutch or English language. • The evaluation report must have been completed 
within ﬁve years prior to the closing date for the submission of Tenders. If the Tenderer uses an assignment 
that is not yet fully complete, then only the completed results of the ongoing assignment can be submitted 
for evaluation purposes: projected results cannot be taken into consideration. Mid-term evaluations and 
draft evaluation reports are allowed, as long as data collection and data analysis have been ﬁnalised. • The 
lead author or team leader of the report is a salaried employee (or owner or self-employed) of the Tenderer 
on the closing date for submission of the Tender; and that author must have contributed to the evaluation.

or without meta-analysis, review of reviews, evidence and gap maps, and mega-maps.
