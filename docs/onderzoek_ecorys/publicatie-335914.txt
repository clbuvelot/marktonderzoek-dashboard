5. Lot
5.1 Lot: LOT-0001
Title:  Lot 1: Evaluation services
Description: 
Lot 1: Evaluation services. This is an overarching term for various types of research at the project and 
portfolio levels. More speciﬁcally, it includes the following services: a. Process evaluations: research on 
the implementation, planning, and management of an intervention/project/programme/subsidy 
framework, without speciﬁcally examining eﬀectiveness; b. Ex-ante studies: analysis of expected 
eﬀects, costs, and outcomes of an intervention/project/programme/subsidy frameworkbefore 
implementation; c. Baseline studies: a baseline measurement of speciﬁc indicators at the beginning of 
an intervention/project/programme/subsidy framework, before implementation; d. Mid-term reviews: 
conducting research on the implementation process and/or the level of eﬀectiveness and/or eﬃciency, 
halfway through the duration of an intervention/project/programme/subsidy framework; e. Eﬀect 
evaluations: assessing the eﬀectiveness, with a clear causal claim, and eﬃciency of a project, in the 
ﬁnal year or after completion of a project; f. Longer evaluation trajectories: researchers are involved 
throughout the entire implementation of an intervention/project/programme/subsidy framework. This 
includes providing independent advice in setting up MEL systems, conducting baseline studies, mid-
term reviews, and ﬁnal (eﬀect) evaluation; g. Portfolio evaluation: an evaluation of an instrument
/subsidy framework/number of thematically or geographically grouped interventions, under which 
various projects are implemented, possibly in multiple countries; h. Policy Evaluation Review (PR); 
these policy evaluations are largely based on previously completed evaluations. For each budget 
article, insights obtained through evaluations should be compiled at least once every four to seven 
years in a PR (in accordance with Regeling periodiek evaluatieonderzoek 2022).
