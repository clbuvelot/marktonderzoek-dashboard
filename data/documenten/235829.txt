Background information In 2019, the Dutch Ministry of the Foreign Affairs (NL MFA) has
launched PROSPECTS, a multiannual hybrid project/partnership between five multilateral
organisations, with the institutional goal of furthering the transformation of the ongoing
responses to protracted forced displacement in some of the most affected countries in the
world.
PROSPECTS brings together organisations working across the humanitarian-development
spectrum: United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), United Nations
Children’s Fund (UNICEF), International Labour Organisation (ILO), International Finance
Corporation (IFC), and the World Bank (WB). The Government of the Netherlands has
committed to allocate USD 558 million in total for this partnership over the course of 5 years
PROSPECTS is operational in 8 countries of the Middle East and the Horn of Africa:
Iraq, Lebanon, Jordan, Egypt, Ethiopia, Sudan, Uganda and Kenya. Programmatically, it
focuses on three key areas: protection, education and livelihoods, as well as all relevant
cross-cutting issues. Intervention modalities and levels of operational cooperation per
country are decided by the five partners, who work on the basis of the joint Multi-Annual
Country Programmes (MACPs), updated on yearly basis. Country-level operations are
