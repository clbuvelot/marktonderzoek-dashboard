16 Selection Guideline - 1.3.4 Volume indication & 1.3.5 Estimated value
Vraag:
An indication of the number of PE-connections used annually by both DSOs together is 
approximately 26,000 pieces within the primary diameter range (63mm to 200mm). Based on the 
information currently available, the DSOs estimate the total value of this Tender Procedure at 
approximately € 17,520,000 for the duration of both Agreements. To accommodate for 
uncertainties and future developments, a bandwidth is applied with a range between € 15,000,000 
and € 20,000,000.
We have two questions regarding the bandwith: 
Nota van inlichtingen

• Could you indicate on which data the DSO’s arrived at the value/bandwidth of this contract? 
• Will the bandwidth be in- or decreased if during the development phase it becomes apparent 
 that the threshold is too high or low?
Antwoord:
The estimated bandwidth is based on historical usage data, projected grid expansion, and the 
estimated costs for development and the unit price of the new couplings. Regarding the 
adjustment of this bandwidth: the published maximum threshold of € 20,000,000 serves as the 
ﬁnancial ceiling for this tender in accordance with European procurement regulations. While the 
ﬁnal contract value can be lower depending on the outcome of the development phase and actual 
call-oﬀs, the maximum threshold cannot be increased, as this would constitute a material change 
to the published scope.
