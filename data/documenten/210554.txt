II.1.4) Short description
As a result of the expiry of current agreements, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs is going to
tender ‘Data delivery services for cost of living, location rating and housing’. The purpose of
the Tendering Process is to conclude an Agreement with one (1) or two (2) service providers
for data delivery services. The intention is for both Agreements to commence on the 1st of
December.
We have divided the Tendering Process into two (2) lots. These lots are:
1) lot 1: Data on ‘cost of living indices’ and ‘location rating data’;
2) lot 2: Data regarding the ‘costs of accommodation per location’ (housing).
