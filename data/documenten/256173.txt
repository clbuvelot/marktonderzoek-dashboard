Precise information on deadline(s) for review procedures:
The standstill period commences the day after publication of the Award Decision on CTM.
The standstill period will last twenty (20) calendar days. During this period, you have the
opportunity to take legal measures against the Award Decision. The standstill period serves
as a strict deadline. After the standstill period, you will no longer be able to object to the
Award Decision because your rights will have ceased to exist. We are then free to enter into
an Agreement. You can read more about the standstill period in Article 2.127 of the Public
Procurement Act 2012.
