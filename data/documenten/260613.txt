II.1.4) Short description
Doel van de aanbesteding: de Opdracht
Royal Schiphol Group is looking for a company or consortium that can help us deliver a
climate adaptation strategy and implementation plan.
Mobility connects people and cities, and airports provide vital infrastructure for connectivity.
Airports are also safe havens to supply food, humanitarian and emergency services; like
being a military basis, providing emergency medical and rescue helicopter services and
facilitating cargo logistics that are crucial for emergencies. To continue these services,
and protect the benefits of global travel and trade, it is crucial that - besides decarbonising
aviation - we create resilient, futureproof airports.
