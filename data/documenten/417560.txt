A02.008.2025 Feasibility Study for Urban Mobility in Abidjan, Ivory Coast
Korte beschrijving van de 
aanbesteding:
The purpose of this tender is to select 1 Contractor who will be responsible for performing a 
feasibility study - including an ESIA - for the development of urban mobility on the Ebrié 
Lagoon in Abidjan, Ivory Coast. The primary objective this study is to enhance urban 
mobility and improve quality of life for residents in Greater Abidjan, by expanding the ﬂeet 
of environmentally friendly boat-buses on the Ebrié Lagoon.

statistische doeleinden. Micro-ondernemingen: een onderneming met minder dan 10 werknemers en met een 
jaaromzet en/of jaarlijks balanstotaal van niet meer dan 2 miljoen euro. Kleine ondernemingen: een onderneming

euro; middelgrote ondernemingen, ondernemingen die micro noch klein zijn en met minder dan 250 personen 
werknemers en een jaaromzet van niet meer dan 50 miljoen euro en/of een jaarlijks balanstotaal van niet meer 
dan 43 miljoen euro.

Deel III A: Gronden die verband houden met strafrechtelijke veroordelingen
 Gronden in verband met strafrechtelijke veroordelingen krachtens nationale bepalingen waarbij de in artikel 57,

Deelneming aan een criminele organisatie
In de zin van artikel 2 van Kaderbesluit 2008/841/JBZ van de Raad van 24 oktober 2008 ter bestrijding van 
georganiseerde criminaliteit (PB L 300 van 11.11.2008, blz. 42).
Omkoping
In de zin van artikel 3 van de Overeenkomst ter bestrijding van corruptie waarbij ambtenaren van de 
Europese Gemeenschappen of van de lidstaten van de Europese Unie betrokken zijn (PB C 195 van 
25.6.1997, blz. 1) en artikel 2, lid 1, van Kaderbesluit 2003/568/JBZ van 22 juli 2003 inzake de bestrijding 
van corruptie in de privésector (PB L 192 van 31.7.2003, blz. 54). Deze uitsluitingsgrond omvat ook 
corruptie in de zin van de nationale wetgeving van de aanbestedende dienst (aanbestedende entiteit) of de 
ondernemer.
Fraude
In de zin van artikel 1 van de Overeenkomst aangaande de bescherming van de ﬁnanciële belangen van de 
Europese Gemeenschappen (PB C 316 van 27.11.1995, blz. 48).
Witwassen van geld of ﬁnanciering van terrorisme
In de zin van artikel 1 van Richtlijn 2005/60/EG van het Europees Parlement en de Raad van 26 oktober 
2005 tot voorkoming van het gebruik van het ﬁnanciële stelsel voor het witwassen van geld en de 
ﬁnanciering van terrorisme (PB L 309 van 25.11.2005, blz. 15).
Terroristische misdrijven of strafbare feiten in verband met terroristische activiteiten
In de zin van de artikelen 1 en 3 van Kaderbesluit 2002/475/JBZ van de Raad van 13 juni 2002 inzake 
terrorismebestrijding (PB L 164 van 22.6.2002, blz. 3). Deze uitsluitingsgrond omvat ook uitlokking van, 
medeplichtigheid aan of poging tot het plegen van een strafbaar feit als bedoeld in artikel 4 van genoemd 
kaderbesluit.
Kinderarbeid en andere vormen van mensenhandel
In de zin van artikel 2 van Richtlijn 2011/36/EU van het Europees Parlement en de Raad van 5 april 2011 
inzake de voorkoming en bestrijding van mensenhandel en de bescherming van slachtoﬀers daarvan, en 
ter vervanging van Kaderbesluit 2002/629/JBZ van de Raad (PB L 101 van 15.4.2011, blz. 1).

1 Could you please confirm whether the indicated budget, as referenced in the ToR/RFP, should be considered a fixed ceiling or an

The maximum budget  for this assignment is € 900.000, excluding VAT. Bids exceeding this maximum 
budget, will be excluded from the Tender Procedure. Please also refer to section 4.5 of the Tender 
document and section 1.4 of the Program of Requirements.
2 Will you consider moving the deadline for submitting proposals forward? We believe the remaining time between the queastions 
being answered and the deadline is too short for a good quality proposal.
The deadline for submitting proposals is extended to 26 September 2025, 10:00 AM. The time 
schedule has been updated accordingly in TenderNed:
3 We understand the requirement for a consultant certified under ISO 9001:2015. However, we would like to question the relevance 
of the ISO 27001:2013 certification, which pertains to information security management systems, in the context of a study that 
does not involve IT systems. Could this requirement be reconsidered or removed?
This certification goes beyond information security management systems, as it focusses on protecting 
all valuable information within an organisation, regardless of whether it is stored digitally or 
physically. An ISO 27001 or equivalent to these standards is required. This means that a Tenderer 
meets this requirement when it has i.e. a policy and procedures in place in order to protect valuable 
information as listed in section C of Appendix 1 - Suitability requirements.
4 The RfP specifies that the maritime civil engineer must have at least 10 years of experience in the conceptualization, 
construction, and supervision of maritime infrastructure for public transportation. However, the expertise required to design 
infrastructure for freight transport is largely similar. Would it be possible to broaden the requirement to: “10 years of experience in 
conceptualization, construction, and supervision of maritime infrastructure”?
The expert position requirement should be interpreted as needing to have minimum of 10 years of 
experience in conceptualization, construction, and supervision of maritime infrastructure for 
passanger transport. 
5 In the Program of Requirements document, RS8 mentions that a disbursement plan must be provided. Could you please clarify 
what is meant by a "disbursement plan" in this context? Are you referring to a payment schedule?
The disbursement plan is the payment schedule by which IIPP disburses payments to the contractor. A 
proposal of this payment schedule is presented in article 3.6 of the draft ageement. 
6 Will the preliminary analysis of E&S impacts (page 15 of the ToR) cover all sites, only those selected following the technical study, 
or just the final chosen site?
At this stage, we do not anticipate that a precise selection among the identified sites will have been 
thoroughly carried out. Therefore, the preliminary analysis should be a quick scan of all sites, 
identifying future challenges and specificites that will be further analyzed. However, if certain sites are 
excluded by then, in agreement with AMUGA, it will not be necessary to include them.
7 Appendix 3 mentions as a budget 1.000.000 euro (excl VAT), Appendix 5 mentions 900.000 euro. Can you confirm the budget is 
900.000 euro?
The maximum budget  for this assignment is € 900.000, excluding VAT. See our answer to question 1. 
8 Can you confirm which of the quoted prices mentioned in RT2 in appendix 5 is used to assess and rate the financial bids? The financial bids - and therefore the Award Criterion 'Price' - will be assessed excluding VAT. 
9 In RCT4 in appendix 5 specifies for expert position 2 ‘Maritime civil engineer’ that the person should have a Minimum 10 years' 
experience in conceptualization, construction and supervision of maritime infrastructures for public transportation. As maritime 
public transport is rare, can this be interpreted as maritime infrastructures for passenger transport?
The expert position requirement should be interpreted as needing to have minimum of 10 years of 
experience in conceptualization, construction, and supervision of maritime infrastructure for 
passanger transport.
