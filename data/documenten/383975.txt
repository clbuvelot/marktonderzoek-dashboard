2 Invitation document
Vraag:
In the tender document is not specified which kind of sensors must be used 
in order to make the quotation for the bidding. Which sensors must tenderer 
use?
Antwoord:
Indeed, specific sensors are not mentioned in the tender document, with the 
exception of three pre-determined instruments (Pi-10, CytoSense and 
LabSTAF) which were selected based on input from the main data users. 
There are several reasons for this. 
a) At the time of writing the tender document, insufficient information was 
available regarding the specifications of the (other) required sensors 
(precision, resolution, etc.). 
b) Determining sensor specifications in consultation with data users, 
selecting suitable sensors based on these specifications, and evaluating 
maintenance needs and financial implications requires experience, expertise 
and potentially some creativity. This task is better handled by an experienced 
market party and is therefore included as part of the assignment in the tender 
document (section 3.1.1). 
The costs for sensors not specified in the requirements specification do NOT 
need to be included in the tenderer's bid. This creates a level playing field 
between tenderers. Similarly, the Pi-10 and CytoSense, which have already 
been purchased by Rijkswaterstaat, do not need to be included in the bid. 
However, the LabSTAF is a pre-determined instrument, but has not yet been 
purchased by Rijkswaterstaat and therefore must be included in the 
tenderer's offer. As specified in section 3.1.1, the Contractor must organize a 
meeting with Rijkswaterstaat (with end users present) to finalize sensor 
selection, where choices must be translated into:
• Financial implications (purchase + maintenance)
• Impact on service, maintenance and calibration
• Accuracy, precision, resolution and range

4 Tender documents
Vraag:
For the data quality and data quality delivery, must the data be checked by 
the contractor before sending or must the data be transmitted in real live?
Antwoord:
Since the Contractor is responsible for the proper performance and 
maintenance of the FerryBox and sensors, Rijkswaterstaat assumes that the 
Contractor will also need to perform the data quality control that is directly 
linked to the performance and maintenance of sensors, instruments and 
peripheral equipment.
The quality control framework specified in section 3.4.4 (following QC 
levels 0-3 as defined by SMHI) is included in the tender document to 
facilitate discussion about the division of responsibilities between the 
Contractor, Rijkswaterstaat, and involved researchers regarding data quality 
control. The framework is indicative and intended to support discussions 
about the specific roles and responsibilities in data quality control after the 
contract has been awarded.
