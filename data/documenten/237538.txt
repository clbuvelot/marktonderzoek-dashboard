II.1.4) Short description
This assignment focuses on the baseline, monitoring and mid-term evaluation of the “Policy
Framework for Strengthening Civil Society” . Under this grant instrument, funding has
been availed for civil society organisations until December 2025. The grant instrument is
subdivided into 6 grant instruments all focusing on strengthening civil society and lobby &
advocacy. 42 partnerships started their 5-year programmes in January 2021, in 65 countries.
The baseline, TPM & DQA and midterm review will be performed in Burkina Faso,
Lebanon, Occupied Palestinian Territories (OPT), South Sudan and Uganda. On top of the
country level studies, which are divided in three research lots, a fourth research lot will focus
on the global component of the various programs. All partnerships active in the selected
country/international component will be included in the evaluation.
