II.1.4) Short description
The Dutch Ministry of Foreign Affairshas previously tendered the baseline, Third Party
Monitoring & Data Quality Assessment and the Mid-Term Review for five countries. We
were unable to award lot 1, the assignment for Uganda and South Sudan due to invalid
tenders. Therefore we have decided to re-tender this part.
This assignment focuses on the baseline, monitoring and mid-term evaluation of the “Policy
Framework for Strengthening Civil Society” for Uganda and South Sudan . Under this grant
instrument, funding has been availed for civil society organisations until December 2025. The
grant instrument is subdivided into 6 grant instruments all focusing on strengthening civil
society and lobby & advocacy. 42 partnerships started their 5-year programmes in January
2021, in 65 countries. All partnerships active in the selected countries will be included in the
evaluation.

VI.3) ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
The Contracting Authority wishes to retain the option of further expanding its services. This
does not change the general nature and scope of the Assignment, the service provision will
remain the same. This includes the possibility of extra third party monitoring & data quality
assessments and/or executing an end evaluation. The decision as to whether this will be used
depends on, among other things, the findings from the MTR and political factors. It is not
clear at this moment when a decision will be made on this. This will be done in time, so that
the Client and the Contractor can discuss the possible consequences for the Assignment. If
the Contracting Authority decides to use this option, the Assignment will be extended with
one or two years.
