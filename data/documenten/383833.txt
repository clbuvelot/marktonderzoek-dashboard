2.1 Procedure
Title: Highly experienced water management and flood management expert
Description: 
The expert provides high level technical expertise contribution to enhance Invest 
International activities for development of implementation of integrated solutions for 
complex water management challenges and (larger) water infrastructure. The expert 
has more than 15 years of experience in flood management, coastal protection, river 
basin management and nature-based solutions developed in the Netherlands and in 
international projects in Asia and Africa. The expert has working experience in IF and 
proven experience in steering large consultancy projects (through TOR’s, tender 
evaluation, progress monitoring, reviewing deliverables). The expert knows how to 
work with the DRIVE and D2B Programmes of Invest International. Furthermore, the 
expert is/will not be involved in any way in assignments of other consultants, who 
perform (or wish to perform) assignments for Invest International. Framework 
agreement with a term of 4 years.
