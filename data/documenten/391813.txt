Introduction  
Scope document 
This document details the Scope of Work (SoW) for soil expert support services for (parts 
of) Search Areas 6&7,  an area in the Dutch North Sea  which will be investigated for the 
development of Offshore Wind.  
 
Background 
The Ministry of Climate Policy and Green Growth (Klimaat en Groene Groei) is responsible 
for the legislative framework for the development of offshore wind farms in the 
Netherlands. Within this framework (a) (concession) tender(s) for construction and 
operation of (a) wind farm(s) will be organi sed. As part of the tender documentation, the 
participants will receive an information package in which detailed information on the 
offshore site is included. Detailed information about soil and wind & water conditions at the 
site will be part of this information package. The Contracting Authority for this assignment 
is the Netherlands Enterprise Agency (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland - RVO), 
sometimes also referred to as Contracting Authority.  
 
Objective 
RVO has been requested to prepare and collect all site data required for commercial 
developers to prepare a competitive bid. In order to achieve the highest results from the 
site characterisation, RVO intends to contract high quality experts who can support RVO in 
managing the site characterisation. The objective of this tender is to conclude an agreement 
with Contractors who can support RVO on the different site characterisations  regarding 
contracting, quality control and operational support.  
 
RVO is looking for an expert support Contractor who can support RVO with managing the 
site characterisations and the interfaces between the site characterisations. RVO is 
specifically looking for expertise in the field of soil characterisation (Lot 1) and ecology (Lot 
2). 
 
Annex 5a, or this SoW, describes the works included for Lot 1: expert support services for 
soil characterisation.  
 
Site Description 
Within the Dutch Shelf of the North Sea several search areas have been selected with 
potential for the development of offshore wind. Search area  (SA) 6/7 is located 
approximately 94 km off the northwest coast of the Netherlands. There is no designated 
wind farm zone in this area yet. A wind farm zone in area 6/7 will  be designated in the 
Partial Revision of the North Sea Programme, which is expected mid -2025. The aim is to 
find space for 23 -26 GW of wind farms  total. The search area has a total surface area of 
approximately 4638 km2. This tender relates to quality expert support for part of SA 6/7, 
encompassing an area of around 800-1000 km2, or about 6-10 GW.

The Contracting Authority intends to conclude the contract with one Contractor, in order to

1) Geological Desk studies. 
 
2) Site characterisation of an area of approximately 800-1000 km2 or ~6-10GW; 
o Geophysical survey(s) 
o Geotechnical survey(s) 
o Ground model (s) 
o Morphodynamical assessment (s) 
 
3) Optional (Non-specified) expert support for works on various sites, other than 
6&7. Expert support might aid RVO with: 
o Geophysical campaign  
o Geotechnical campaign  
o Ground model works  
o Morphodynamical assessment  
 
The content of the site characterisation for the search areas, will be based on forthcoming 
political decisions. This may result in the fact that Contracting Authority and expert 
support will have to take a pragmatic approach to the investigations.  
  
Contracting Authority foresees that site characterisation surveys between the above 
mentioned items might be combined, or run simultaneously.

Doordewind 
DDW is located to the North -East border of the Dutch shelf of the North Sea. The site is 
located North-West of the Ten noorden van de Waddeneilanden Wind Farm Zone. In total 
it is expected that this site can accommodate a maximum of 4 GW of offshore wind divided 
over multiple sites. Figure 1 below is presenting the different wind farm zones and search 
areas, including SA 6/7 and DDW.   
 
Search area 6/7 
Search area (SA) 6/7 is located approximately 94 km off the northwest coast of the 
Netherlands. There is no designated wind farm zone in this area yet. A wind farm zone in 
area 6/7 will be designated in the Partial Revision of the North Sea Programme, which is 
expected mid-2025. The aim is to find space for 23-26 GW of wind farms total. The search 
area has a tot al surface area of approximately 4638 km2. This tender relates to quality 
expert support for part of SA 6/7, encompassing an area of around 800-1000 km2, or about 
6-10 GW.

b. General knowledge of the North Sea ecosystem, above and below water; 
c. General knowledge of measuring techniques for different species (groups);  
d. Insight in the ecological data required for wind farm engineering design.
