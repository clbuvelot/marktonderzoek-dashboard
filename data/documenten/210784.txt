penalty of the complaint being declared inadmissible and the loss of rights, must file for
interim injunction proceedings against this decision of the Contracting Authority before the
(Provisional Relief Section of the) District Court of East Brabant within a term of seven
(7) (calendar) days of the dispatch date of the notification of this decision. If no interim
injunction proceedings are filed within seven (7) (calendar) days of the dispatch of the notice
of the decision, the Candidate cannot file any further objections to the decision and loses its
rights in that regard. In that case, therefore, the Contracting Authority is free to follow up
the reported decision. The instigation of an interim injunction action by the Candidate has
no consequences for the current Follow-up RFPs or Follow-up RFPs still to be started by the
Contracting Authority.
