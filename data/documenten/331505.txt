The Dutch Metropolitan Innovations (DMI-) ecosystem serves the disciplines 
mobility, public space, energy, ecology and housing in the Netherlands utilizing 
new solutions of the digital world. In the coming years, the Ministry of 
Infrastructure and Water Management, in collaboration with other public 
entities, shall regularly award so-called innovation, research and development 
assignments to private organizations that are, or will become participants in the 
DMI-ecosystem. 1,00
